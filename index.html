While presenting

USB clickers that act like a keyboard work: Page Down or Enter advances; Page Up or Backspace goes back. Press B for black screen or W for white screen. F5 (often the clicker’s start button) shows or hides the church logo instead of restarting the app. Ctrl/Command+R is blocked during a presentation.

Getting started

Learn how to prepare a service and present it safely.

Help and guidance

Build a service

Use Add slide at the bottom of the slide list to add content at the end. Use Order of Sections to insert and arrange content at a particular point.

Selecting, editing and presenting

Selecting a slide changes the operator preview. Editing changes saved service content. Press Present only when you intend to send the current slide to the audience display.

Songs, media and imported slides

Use Songs to browse or add songs, Media to manage reusable media, and Import to add a presentation or PDF. Content is added only when you choose an add action.

Saving and audience display

Service changes save automatically on this computer. Google Drive sync is available when connected and configured. Choose the audience display in Settings → Presentation; the audience window is separate from the operator view.
