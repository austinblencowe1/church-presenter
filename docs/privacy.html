Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Overview

Church Presenter is a desktop application for preparing and presenting church services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our app works.

Data Collection and Storage

Church Presenter is primarily a local desktop application. Your service data, presentations, songs, and media are stored locally on your device in the application's data directory.

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on our servers. There is no analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking built into the app.

Google Drive Integration

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the following applies:

  • What we access: Church Presenter requests access to your Google Drive with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope. This allows the app to create, read, and write files in folders that you explicitly select and grant access to.
  • What we do with it: We only access files and folders that you choose to sync with Church Presenter (service files, templates, and media you designate for sharing). The app creates and manages files within a Drive folder that you select.
  • Where data goes: All synced data remains between your Google Drive account and your local device. Data is not sent to any third-party servers other than Google's services as part of normal Google Drive API usage.
  • Authentication: We use OAuth 2.0 with PKCE for secure authentication. Refresh tokens are stored securely in your system's keychain (macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager/DPAPI).

Revoking Access

You can revoke Church Presenter's access to your Google Drive at any time by:

  1. Going to your Google Account Permissions page
  2. Finding "Church Presenter" in the list of third-party apps
  3. Clicking "Remove Access"

You can also disconnect from Google Drive directly within the app via the Google Drive settings.

Third-Party Services

Church Presenter uses the Google Drive API when you opt in to cloud sync. Use of Google services is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Security

All authentication tokens are stored using platform-specific secure storage mechanisms. Local data remains on your device and is not transmitted unless you explicitly use the Google Drive sync feature.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
