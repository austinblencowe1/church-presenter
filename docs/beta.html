Beta Testing

Church Presenter is currently in beta testing while we go through Google's OAuth verification process. Anyone can test the app, but there is one small step if you use Google Drive sync.

Google Drive Sync Note

When connecting Google Drive for the first time, you'll see a screen that says the app is not verified yet and may be unsafe. This is normal while verification is in progress. Simply click Advanced, then Go to Church Presenter (unsafe), and continue through the prompts to grant access. The app should be fully verified soon.
